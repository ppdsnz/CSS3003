15-minute learning defense

Defend the Office Core from bad software decisions.

You are the commander of the Office Fortress. Each enemy represents a real application-software decision. Correct decisions fire the knowledge tower. Wrong decisions let the enemy advance and reduce your mark.

Learning Objective
By the end of the game, students can select the most appropriate application software, office productivity tool, DBMS component, AI office feature, or ethical action for a given situation.
Wave 1 • Productivity GateCore application software, Word, spreadsheets, presentations and integration.
Wave 2 • Data BastionDBMS concepts, Microsoft Access components and data-management decisions.
Wave 3 • AI CitadelAI office integration, prompt use, limitations, privacy and human oversight.
Assessment rule: Each of the 20 questions begins at 5 marks. Every wrong attempt reduces that question by 1 mark, down to a minimum of 1 mark. The red enemy moves closer to the blue defense tower after every wrong answer. If it reaches the blue tower, the fortress is breached and the game is over. You may then restart the game. Maximum final mark = 100.

Wrong answers reveal no hint or correct answer. Explanations appear only after a correct response. Question order and answer choices are shuffled for each new game, while Easy → Medium → Hard progression is preserved.
